TL;DR

Four open decision areas — contact placement, tech-stack section, "About this org", project order/density — each have 4 genuine alternatives. Recommended combination: keep the top+bottom contact bookend (add the calendar link to both), repurpose the standalone tech-stack badges as an accurate cross-project signal moved higher, replace "About this org" with one honest hook sentence, and keep coupon-service first while compressing both projects' "Demonstrates" lines into the same punchy-lead-plus-detail shape. None of these are locked — convergence should confirm or override each independently.

Key Decisions (leans, not locked)

  • 1A — keep contact bookend (top badges + bottom repeat, add calendar to both) — matches the user's own stated preference.
  • 2B — repurpose tech-stack badges as an accurate cross-project row, moved higher — cutting would contradict the stated "badge scanning" preference; keeping as-is would misrepresent skill-flip's real stack.
  • 3B — replace "About this org" with one honest hook sentence — matches assumptions.md point 4 almost verbatim.
  • 4B — keep order (coupon-service first), compress both "Demonstrates" lines into a punchy-lead + detail shape — fixes scan speed without the framing-mismatch risk of reordering.

Open Questions / Risks

  • 1A keeps some redundancy by design — if unwanted once rendered, fall back to 1D (asymmetric: full badges top, single calendar nudge bottom).
  • Repositioning tech-stack badges (2B) is a bigger structural change than a same-spot edit — confirm this restructuring latitude is acceptable, else fall back to 2A (cut).
  • Compressing coupon-service's five-item list (4B) must keep the specific keywords (WebFlux, Testcontainers, R2DBC) in the secondary detail clause, not just a vague headline.
  • The four areas interact — e.g. cutting "About this org" entirely (3A) removes the only page-level home for the honesty disclosure the other leans assume exists.
